Chapter 2: Managing Form Submissions & Reports

Core Functionality

The website has 5 active interactive forms: Report a Case, Contact Us, Join the Mission, Share an Idea, and Track Status. Whenever a visitor submits any form, two things happen instantly:

  • The submission is permanently logged in your WordPress Admin database.
  • An immediate email alert with full details is dispatched to amnnisafoundation@gmail.com.

Viewing Submissions in WP Admin:

A
Access via Left Menu or Top Admin Bar
Click on Submissions in the left sidebar, or click the top shortcut 🌸 Amnnisa Submissions (X) from anywhere on the site.
B
Understanding the Submissions Table
Column Meaning & Handling
Reference ID Unique tracking code (e.g. AN-2026-LON81) given to the submitter so they can track their report privately.
Type The form submitted (Case report, Contact message, Join mission, New idea).
Submitter Name and contact info. If the user checked "Report Anonymously", it clearly displays 🛡 Anonymous.
Urgency Indicates priority: Emergency (Red badge), High (Pink badge), or Standard.
Details & Message Click on ► View submitted details to expand and read the full report, location, and answers.
Status Displays ✓ Sent if delivered to Gmail, or Logged if saved locally.
Actions Click Resend to re-dispatch the email alert, or Delete to remove the record.
🔒 Confidentiality Protocol: In accordance with Amnnisa's mission, treat all case reports with strict privacy. Do not disclose anonymous submitter details or IP information to third parties.

Chapter 3: Setting Up Live Email Delivery (Gmail SMTP)

Email Alerts

Standard web hosts block basic PHP mail() or cause emails to land in spam. Amnnisa includes a built-in SMTP engine that connects directly to Google's official mail servers using a secure 16-letter Google App Password.

1
Generate Google App Password (2 Minutes)
  1. Log into your Google account: amnnisafoundation@gmail.com.
  2. Open: https://myaccount.google.com/security
  3. Ensure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.
  4. Go directly to: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  5. Under "App name", type: Amnnisa Website and click Create.
  6. Google will display a yellow box with a 16-letter password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy this code.
2
Enter Settings in WordPress Customizer
In your WordPress Admin, go to Appearance → Customize → Amnnisa Site Content & Sections → 12. SMTP Email Delivery:
Setting Field Exact Value to Enter
Enable Custom SMTP Delivery ☑ Check this box
SMTP Host smtp.gmail.com
SMTP Port 587
Encryption tls
SMTP Username amnnisafoundation@gmail.com
SMTP App Password Paste your 16-letter Google code here
From Email Address amnnisafoundation@gmail.com
From Name Display Amnnisa Platform
Click the blue Publish button at the top.
3
Test with 1 Click
Go to Submissions in your left menu and click the button at the top:

👉 ✉ Send Test Email to amnnisafoundation@gmail.com

A green checkmark will appear confirming that email delivery is working 100%!

Chapter 4: Managing Team Members

Team Page

The Amnnisa theme features a dedicated Team Members post type. All 6 founding members are already pre-loaded into your database. You can edit their roles, change photos, delete anyone, or add new members anytime.

1
Editing an Existing Team Member
  1. Go to Team Members in your left menu.
  2. Hover over any member (e.g. Aliza Shah) and click Edit.
  3. Edit their Name in the title box.
  4. Under Team Member Details & Photo, edit:
    • Title / Role (e.g. Co-Founder)
    • Department / Function (e.g. Marketing & PR)
  5. Change Photo: Either upload a new photo in the Featured Image box on the right, OR paste a direct URL into the Photo Image URL box.
  6. Click Update. Your changes will immediately reflect on the live website.
2
Adding a New Team Member
  1. Go to Team Members → Add New Member.
  2. Enter the member's full name in the title field.
  3. Fill in their Role and Department.
  4. Upload their photo in the Featured Image box (Square 1:1 ratio recommended, e.g. 400×400px).
  5. Click Publish. The new member card appears instantly in the team grid.
3
Deleting or Reordering Members
  • To Remove a Member: Hover over their name in the Team list and click Trash.
  • To Reorder Members: In the member edit screen, expand Page Attributes on the right and adjust the Order number (1, 2, 3, etc.).
  • To Reset Defaults: Click the ↺ Restore Default 6 Members button in the notice banner at the top of the Team Members screen.
💡 Founder Spotlight: Homair Nisar's spotlight card at the top of the Team page is managed under Appearance → Customize → 9. Our Team — Founder Spotlight.

Chapter 6: Emergency Hotlines & One-Touch Calling

Life Safety

On mobile devices, every emergency hotline card is programmed as an instant tap-to-call link (tel:15, tel:1099, etc.). If a woman in danger taps on Police 15, her phone immediately dials the police emergency helpline.

To update emergency phone numbers: Go to Appearance → Customize → 6. Home — Emergency Hotlines.

Chapter 8: Security & Performance Highlights

Protection
Security Feature How It Protects Your Platform
Invisible Honeypot All forms contain an invisible trap field. Spambots automatically fill it and get silently dropped, without bothering real users with annoying captchas.
IP Rate Limiting Restricts submissions to 8 per 10 minutes per IP address to prevent form flooding and DDoS attacks.
XML-RPC Disabled Blocks automated password guessing and amplification attacks targeting xmlrpc.php.
Author Enumeration Block Prevents automated scanners from probing /?author=1 to discover administrator usernames.
HTTP Security Headers Enforces modern security headers (X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, etc.) to defend against clickjacking and MIME attacks.

Chapter 9: Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Help & FAQ
?
Why does "Pages" in WP Admin only show Sample Page?
Amnnisa is designed as a modern high-speed single-page application where all sections switch instantly without slow reloads. All content is edited visually in Appearance → Customize and Team Members. To set up your homepage cleanly: create a page named Home (select template: Amnnisa Platform Full Template) and assign it in Settings → Reading.
?
What if an admin email notification is not arriving in Gmail?
Go to Submissions in WP Admin and click Send Test Email. If it displays a notice, follow Chapter 3 to generate a 16-letter Google App Password and enter it under Appearance → Customize → 12. SMTP Email Delivery.
?
How do I change the founder bio or quote?
Open Appearance → Customize. For the quote, click 4. Home — Founder Quote. For the founder portrait and bio, click 9. Our Team — Founder Spotlight.
?
Is the website mobile responsive on iPhone and Android?
Yes. The layout adapts smoothly to all screen sizes from large 4K monitors down to compact 320px mobile screens, featuring app-style horizontal swipeable tabs, touch-friendly buttons, and iOS zoom prevention.
