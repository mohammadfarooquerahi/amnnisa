About Us

Securing women's dignity

Amnnisa is a women's safety and empowerment initiative guiding women on how to respond to threats and harassment through legal, technical, and moral support. Founded by Homair Nisar and incubated at the MUET Business Incubation Center, Jamshoro. We combine empathetic guidance, technical countermeasures, and data-backed advocacy so no woman feels alone or unprotected.

⚖

Legal guidance

Clear direction on rights and the steps to take under the law.

💻

Technical guidance

Practical help for digital safety, account security, and evidence preservation.

💗

Moral support

A private, respectful space to be heard without judgment or exposure.

Common questions

Is my report really private?

Yes. You can report completely anonymously, and your identity is never exposed or shared without your explicit consent.

Do I need proof to report?

No. Proof helps strengthen your case, but you can report first and our team will guide you on how to safely gather and preserve evidence.

Is Amnnisa an emergency service?

No. If you are in immediate physical danger, please call police emergency 15 or 1122 right away before reaching out to us.

Who can join the mission?

Students, volunteers, lawyers, technologists, and organizations passionate about women's safety are welcome to apply via our Get Involved section.

Founder & Vision

Homair Nisar

Founder, Amnnisa

Homair Nisar founded Amnnisa with a single conviction: that every woman deserves to walk through life with dignity, safety, and the full backing of her community. As a student-leader incubated at the MUET Business Incubation Center, he envisions Amnnisa as a bridge between immediate protection and systemic change — leveraging technology, legal literacy, and empathetic care to rewrite how society responds to harassment.
